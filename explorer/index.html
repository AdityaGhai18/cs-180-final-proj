Mask + Depth Explorer

Each photo goes through src/image_pipeline.py with three inputs: the photo, a texture, and a text prompt naming the object. SAM 3 cuts out whatever the prompt describes, Depth Anything V2 estimates how near every pixel is, and the texture is laid inside the mask. These outputs come from a fresh run on Apple silicon (MPS).

Reading the panels

Text prompt. SAM 3 gets only the words and returns every matching instance; the pipeline keeps the highest-confidence mask.

Relative depth. Brighter means closer. Depth Anything gives an ordering, not meters, and the pipeline stretches the 2nd–98th percentile to 0–1 per image, so numbers aren't comparable across photos.

What to look for. —

Texture. The texture is tiled flat and shaded by photo brightness. It ignores depth, which is why it reads as a tint. Wrapping it along the relief you see in 3D is the next step.
